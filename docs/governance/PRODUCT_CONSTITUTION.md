# HIBEYA Akal Budi — Product Constitution

Version: 1.0
Status: Binding product principle

## Purpose

HIBEYA Akal Budi exists to help children develop useful cognitive,
language, social, emotional and practical-life capabilities through
purposeful digital play rooted in Malaysian life and culture.

The product must not optimise for maximum screen time.

Success means that the child learns, practises, explores, communicates,
plays and eventually carries useful learning back into the physical world.

## Core Product Promise

Membina Akal. Menyemai Budi.

Akal Budi must develop both:

- AKAL: thinking, language, numeracy, memory, reasoning, creativity,
  attention and problem-solving.
- BUDI: empathy, manners, emotional understanding, responsibility,
  family relationships, community awareness and practical-life skills.

## Healthy Digital Play

Digital interaction is a means of learning, not the destination.

The product should favour:

digital activity
→ reflection
→ conversation
→ physical-world activity
→ family interaction

Activities should normally be short and purposeful.

The application must not deliberately encourage prolonged continuous use.

## Forbidden Engagement Patterns

Akal Budi must not use:

- infinite scroll for child activities
- autoplay designed to prevent stopping
- loss-aversion streaks
- punishment for leaving
- manipulative countdowns
- loot-box mechanics
- variable-ratio reward systems
- advertising inside child mode
- behavioural advertising
- public child leaderboards
- shame-based feedback
- paid advantage mechanics
- artificial scarcity aimed at children
- excessive notifications to children
- dark patterns intended to increase session duration

## Error Philosophy

Mistakes are learning events.

A wrong answer must never:

- shame the child
- reduce status
- remove earned progress
- create anxiety
- use harsh sound effects
- display aggressive visual feedback

Preferred language:

"Cuba lagi."

"Jom tengok sekali lagi."

"Hampir betul."

## Reward Philosophy

Rewards should reinforce effort and learning without becoming the primary
reason to continue using the product.

Rewards should be:

- predictable
- restrained
- non-monetary
- non-competitive by default
- visually calm
- optional where possible

## Physical-World Extension

Where appropriate, activities should include an optional real-world extension.

Examples:

"Carilah tiga benda berwarna merah di rumah."

"Kira lima sudu bersama Mama atau Ayah."

"Cari daun yang berbeza bentuk di luar rumah."

"Susun kasut mengikut saiz."

The product should deliberately create opportunities to put the device down.

## Malaysian Identity

Malaysia must be part of the underlying world model of Akal Budi,
not a decorative skin.

Content may naturally include:

- Bahasa Melayu
- Malaysian English
- Malaysian homes
- kampung and city environments
- local plants
- local animals
- food
- transport
- weather
- geography
- architecture
- occupations
- family life
- community life
- traditional stories
- arts and craft traditions
- Malaysian values and daily experiences

Cultural representation must be respectful and avoid stereotypes.

Malaysia's ethnic, linguistic, regional and religious diversity must be
represented thoughtfully where relevant.

## Developmental Integrity

Every learning activity must identify:

- intended age band
- developmental objective
- skill being practised
- expected interaction
- estimated duration
- difficulty
- evidence or pedagogical rationale
- whether parent participation is beneficial

No activity may be described as therapeutic, diagnostic or clinically proven
without appropriate professional evidence and review.

## Sensory Design

Akal Budi should be visually attractive without being overstimulating.

Default design principles:

- limited simultaneous movement
- strong visual hierarchy
- generous whitespace
- predictable navigation
- large touch targets
- restrained reward animations
- reduced-motion support
- no unnecessary flashing
- no rapid scene changes
- calm audio levels
- readable typography

## Privacy

Children should not be required to disclose personal information merely to play.

Collect the minimum data necessary.

Prefer:

nickname
age band
language
progress

Avoid unless genuinely necessary:

full legal name
exact birth date
school
home address
precise location
photo
voice recording
biometric information
government identifiers

Parent or guardian accounts control child profiles.

## Commercial Integrity

Revenue must not override child wellbeing.

If a monetisation mechanism conflicts with developmental integrity,
privacy, accessibility or healthy play, the mechanism must change.

The child is the learner.

The parent or guardian is normally the customer.

The product must create genuine value for both.

## Governance Rule

A feature may be technically possible and commercially attractive and still
be rejected.

Child wellbeing, originality, privacy, accessibility and long-term trust
override short-term engagement metrics.
