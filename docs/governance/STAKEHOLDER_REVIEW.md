# HIBEYA Akal Budi — Stakeholder Review Framework

Version: 1.0

Every significant feature should be evaluated through multiple perspectives.

## Child

Does it feel understandable?

Is it enjoyable without being addictive?

Can mistakes be made safely?

Is the interaction suitable for the child's motor and cognitive abilities?

Does the child know what to do?

## Parent or Guardian

Is the value clear?

Can the parent understand what was practised?

Does the product respect family time?

Can the parent control usage?

Is privacy understandable?

Would the parent trust the product?

## Early Childhood / Education

Is the skill meaningful?

Is the age band reasonable?

Does the interaction support learning rather than merely test recall?

Could the activity transfer to real-world understanding?

## Cultural Review

Is Malaysian representation accurate?

Is language natural?

Are communities represented respectfully?

Are historical or cultural claims supportable?

## Accessibility

Can the activity be understood without relying entirely on:

colour
audio
small text
fine motor precision
rapid movement

Does reduced-motion mode remain usable?

Are touch targets appropriately large?

## Developer

Is the feature modular?

Is business logic separated from presentation?

Can it be tested?

Can the provider or technology be replaced?

Does it introduce unnecessary dependencies?

Does it increase privacy or security risk?

## Content Designer

Can new content be created without modifying the game engine?

Is the schema understandable?

Are assets reusable?

Can mistakes be detected automatically?

## Privacy and Security

What data does the feature require?

Why is each field necessary?

Can the same outcome be achieved with less data?

Where is the information stored?

Who can access it?

How is deletion handled?

## Business

Will parents perceive meaningful value?

Does it solve a real problem?

Is the benefit strong enough to justify payment?

Does monetisation conflict with child wellbeing?

## Operations

How will content be corrected?

How will complaints be handled?

How will inappropriate or inaccurate material be withdrawn?

How will incidents be investigated?

## Review Principle

No single stakeholder perspective dominates every decision.

The final design should optimise the system as a whole.
