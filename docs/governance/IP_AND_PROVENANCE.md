# HIBEYA Akal Budi — IP and Provenance Policy

Version: 1.0

## Objective

HIBEYA Akal Budi should build its own intellectual property rather than
depend on imitation of existing children's applications, games or characters.

## Originality Standard

Where practical, Akal Budi should create original:

- characters
- illustrations
- stories
- activity wording
- sound design
- interface compositions
- interaction implementations
- Malaysian fictional worlds
- names
- reward systems
- educational sequences

Ideas and educational concepts may be common.

Distinctive creative expression from another product must not be copied.

## Competitor Research

Competitors may be studied to understand:

- market expectations
- usability
- common problems
- pricing
- parent needs
- educational gaps
- accessibility gaps

Competitor research must not become:

- copying screen layouts
- tracing illustrations
- rewriting distinctive stories
- cloning characters
- reproducing audio
- copying proprietary question banks
- recreating trademarked visual identity
- copying proprietary code

## Asset Provenance

Every non-trivial production asset should eventually have provenance data.

Possible provenance types:

ORIGINAL
COMMISSIONED
LICENSED
PUBLIC_DOMAIN
AI_ASSISTED

## Original

Created specifically for HIBEYA Akal Budi.

Record:

creator
creation date
internal asset ID

## Commissioned

Created by an external creator.

Record:

creator
agreement reference
ownership or licence terms
date

Commercial agreements should clearly address IP ownership.

## Licensed

Record:

source
licence
commercial-use permission
modification permission
attribution requirements
expiry if applicable

No asset may be used simply because it was found through Google Images,
Pinterest, social media or another website.

## Public Domain

Record the evidence supporting public-domain status.

Do not assume that "old" means public domain.

## AI-Assisted Content

AI output must be treated as material requiring human review.

AI-generated or AI-assisted content must be checked for:

- resemblance to recognisable copyrighted characters
- accidental reproduction of logos
- cultural inaccuracies
- anatomy or visual defects
- unsafe content
- inappropriate stereotypes
- factual errors
- inconsistent visual identity

Prompting should avoid instructions to reproduce the distinctive style or
expression of living artists, commercial children's brands or copyrighted
characters.

## Traditional Culture

Traditional Malaysian motifs, stories and cultural practices require
additional care.

Traditional subject matter may be culturally shared while a particular:

photograph
illustration
recording
translation
adaptation
graphic design

may still be protected.

Use original interpretations or appropriately licensed source material.

## Trademark Review

Before commercial launch, perform trademark clearance for:

HIBEYA
AKAL BUDI
major character names
major product names
logos
