# HIBEYA Akal Budi — Research Register

This file records external research and guidance used to inform product
decisions.

It does not imply that HIBEYA Akal Budi is clinically validated.

## AB-RESEARCH-EARLY-PLAY-001

Area:
Healthy digital learning and early-childhood play

Sources:

World Health Organization
Guidelines on physical activity, sedentary behaviour and sleep for children
under 5 years of age.

American Academy of Pediatrics
Guidance on healthy media use, co-viewing, play and child development.

Application within Akal Budi:

- avoid maximising screen duration
- encourage age-appropriate content
- encourage real-world play
- encourage caregiver interaction where useful
- avoid engagement-driven design
- prevent digital activity from unnecessarily crowding out physical play,
  sleep and family interaction

Review status:
Initial internal evidence basis

Pending
